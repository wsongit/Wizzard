# Walking the Bellwake watershed

From Bellmarket the wooden trail sign starts the walk to Long Meadow. The native Unity highroad has 42 district gates across a 1,440 × 1,200 metre survey. The browser prototype uses the earlier 160 × 144 metre field. [Native field and Blender views](native-field.md) Six rows of milestones run from the coast to the belfry. Look for coastal lanterns, lock hoists, market shelters, standing stones, kilns and spires to tell the rows apart.

When a quest sends you onward, walk to the named gate. You can enter another district from the road with E or a controller trigger, look around, and return through its entrance. Quest progress resumes at the destination. The game streams one detailed area at a time. Quitting on the road restores your latest road checkpoint.

Speaking to a resident holds your position and darkens the surroundings. Read the reply and press E or a controller trigger to continue. You can still turn your head.

## Art reference notes

In the supplied daytime village images, you can pick out the windmill above the rooftops before you can read its wall texture. The well marks the lower crossroads. Retaining walls and stairs explain how the two levels connect. The highroad uses the same idea with six district silhouettes and named gates. The terrain mesh carries both the tracks and the grass so their borders meet at shared vertices.

In the forest image, broad olive patches remain visible underneath shorter bright tufts. On the buildings, dark timber and roof tiles break up the pale plaster. Large colour shapes survive at a distance. Small-scale pattern becomes visible on approach. The road uses separate painted materials for turf, soil, foliage, bark, masonry and signs.

The earlier ROM inspection measured 839 static triangle instances in Kakariko room 0 and 1,639 in Hyrule Field room 0. The two rooms used 20 and 36 culling groups respectively. Kakariko includes 32 × 32, 64 × 32 and 16 × 64 tile settings. Those measurements guided the use of simple silhouettes with painted surface detail. The counts cover decoded room geometry. Actors are separate. [ROM measurements and scope](../oot-rom-study.md)
