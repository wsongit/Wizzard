# Painting a place the player can walk around

In the PAL N64 Kakariko shooting gallery, the roof uses a 16 × 32 tile, the roof edge a 32 × 32 tile, the wall a 32 × 64 tile and the shadow around the door a 16 × 32 tile. The sign has its own 32 × 32 picture. A photograph of that building looks detailed because each picture fits a particular part of the building. The same approach works on a Bellwake cottage when the player can stand close enough to look under its eaves. [Shooting-gallery asset declarations](https://github.com/zeldaret/oot/blob/14759abf9abb4295de142cdc1452998630ea70fb/assets/xml/objects/object_spot01_matoya.xml)

## Houses, shops and furniture

**Start with the opening.** Build a wall, recessed door, jambs, lintel and step at player scale. Leave a little of the dark return visible when the door is viewed at an angle. Paint the narrow front of a lintel lengthwise. Give a roof's exposed edge its own horizontal strip with terracotta ends, a narrow shadow and pegged wood below. Keep the stone on the top and face of a threshold continuous through its bevel. The 04:52:01 view in the recording is a useful framing test. The doorway stands out in a small stone room even though the floor and wall have similar brightness. Bellmarket's cottage fascia, doorframes and steps each have fitted artwork. [Bellwake cottage paintings and Blender fitting](../../Tools/bellwake/cottage_edges.py)

**Shop signs.** In the inspected Kakariko bazaar sign, two triangles use one complete 64 × 64 CI4 picture. Two triangles on the potion-shop sign use a 32 × 16 RGBA16 picture. Aspect ratio does much of the work. Build a projecting bracket and a thin sign with thickness, paint the emblem onto each broad face and give the narrow edge a wood finish. On the Bellwake provisioner's stall, the hem, bracket, suspension cords and enamel sign all have depth. Close one eye and lean sideways to check the cords clear the board. [Original signs](https://github.com/zeldaret/oot/blob/14759abf9abb4295de142cdc1452998630ea70fb/assets/xml/objects/object_spot01_matoya.xml) · [Bellwake provisioner](../../Tools/bellwake/wayfarer_art.py)

**A window can show a business.** Original Kakariko selects a day or night window image with the save clock. Both images are 32 × 64. The potion and mask shops have a different construction, with 256 × 256 indexed background panels and palettes. At 01:03:03 the shelf goods frame a merchant's face. At 05:57:03 the curtains and the masks belong to the same shop composition. In VR, put a small original interior painting behind a real sill, crossbars and shutters. In Bellwake, a lamp brightens the colours already in the picture. Dark vessels and shelves keep their silhouettes at night. [Village window selection](https://github.com/zeldaret/oot/blob/14759abf9abb4295de142cdc1452998630ea70fb/src/code/z_scene_table.c#L1128) · [Shop background images](https://github.com/zeldaret/oot/blob/14759abf9abb4295de142cdc1452998630ea70fb/assets/xml/textures/backgrounds.xml) · [Fixed-camera room drawings](https://github.com/zeldaret/oot/blob/14759abf9abb4295de142cdc1452998630ea70fb/src/code/z_room.c#L411)

**Books and drawers.** A little painted rectangle means more when it has a physical ledge above and below it. Keep the spines as separate solids, tint the bindings by shelf, map the decorated leather onto the narrow outward face and leave the page edges pale. Reuse that sheet for a recessed drawer panel or an index cabinet with a different piece of artwork. Turn your head across a whole shelf. The ledges should move against the bindings, while the gilding remains attached to the leather. Flood Ledger, Vellum Walk, Linen Court and the parish office use this layout. [Bindings and Blender face selection](../../Tools/bellwake/shop_paint.py)

## Goods, tools and working yards

**Crates.** The large-crate display list resolves to ten triangles across two 32 × 64 indexed images. One covers two triangles, the other eight. In Blender, start with a box at lifting height. Align the banding on its side and the end grain or slats on its lid. Stack a smaller article with its base seated on that lid. A single crate silhouette can then describe a baker's delivery, a ferryman's rope locker or the printer's paper stock. [Crate vertices, images and collision](https://github.com/zeldaret/oot/blob/14759abf9abb4295de142cdc1452998630ea70fb/assets/xml/objects/object_kibako2.xml) · [Bellwake workyard construction](../../Tools/bellwake/workyards.py)

**Pots, buckets and casks.** The intact pot fragment in the PAL reference uses the 32 × 64 picture on 28 triangles and a 16 × 16 picture on two. The bank also declares a second small 16 × 16 image. For a new vessel, revolve a few rings at the foot, belly, shoulder and mouth. Walk the body UV around its circumference. Fit a separate picture to the top and give the lip thickness so a crouched player can see inside. On a cask, run the painted grain along the staves and fit a circular head inside the hoops. On the little workshop jars, let a pale rim break the dark mouth. [Pot definition](https://github.com/zeldaret/oot/blob/14759abf9abb4295de142cdc1452998630ea70fb/assets/xml/objects/object_tsubo.xml) · [Bellwake goods paintings](../../Tools/bellwake/workyard_paint.py)

**Mechanisms.** In the inspected Hyrule Field drawbridge chain, the bolt has two triangles mapped to a 16 × 16 image. The chain's four triangles use a 16 × 32 image and cover about 31.75 units of UV area in total. UV area measures the amount of image repeated across those triangles. Stand close to a Bellwake rope reel or crank and the attachment should still appear solid. Build the hub, working handle and visible winding as geometry. Reuse a repeating grain or metal strip on the long members, with a separate end treatment. The winding drum and handle need a common mechanical axis. [Drawbridge assets](https://github.com/zeldaret/oot/blob/14759abf9abb4295de142cdc1452998630ea70fb/assets/xml/objects/object_spot00_objects.xml) · [Bellwake packed mechanism paintings](../../Tools/bellwake/hardware_trims.py)

**A grounded grouping.** At 06:38:01 the rider and horse have a visible patch of shadow beneath them on the pale field. The original actor code can orient a shadow to the floor surface, adjust it for the actor's height or draw separate foot shadows. Its shared bank lists a 32 × 32 circle and a 32 × 64 foot. For stationary goods, Bellwake traces from the actual painted ground towards the placed meshes and bakes a short contact shadow into the pigment. A pot under a tall crate handle and the empty ground beside it then receive different treatment. Moving an entire stock group and rebaking restores the old patch of brushwork. [Actor shadows](https://github.com/zeldaret/oot/blob/14759abf9abb4295de142cdc1452998630ea70fb/src/code/z_actor.c#L79) · [Shadow image declarations](https://github.com/zeldaret/oot/blob/14759abf9abb4295de142cdc1452998630ea70fb/assets/xml/objects/gameplay_keep.xml#L1305) · [Bellwake contact painting](../../Tools/bellwake/stock_contacts.py)

**A windmill sail needs an axle.** The original Kakariko sails have sixteen vertices and eight triangles. A single 32 × 64 RGBA16 image runs along each of the four blades, covering exactly four full images in the measured display list. In Blender, build the hub at a deliberate height above the path. Hang four long spars on the same centre and paint the sailcloth lengthwise. Leave enough space between cloth panels for the sky to show through at a distance. Animate the axle, then walk to both sides of the mill and check the blades stay clear of the tower. Bellmarket's sails share a pivot with the visible hub. [N64 sail and well assets](https://github.com/zeldaret/oot/blob/14759abf9abb4295de142cdc1452998630ea70fb/assets/xml/objects/object_spot01_objects.xml) · [Original sail motion](https://github.com/zeldaret/oot/blob/14759abf9abb4295de142cdc1452998630ea70fb/src/overlays/actors/ovl_Bg_Spot01_Fusya/z_bg_spot01_fusya.c) · [Bellwake hill mill](../../Tools/bellwake/wayside_landmarks.py)

**Build the mouth of a well in section.** In the village reference, the timber arch has twenty-six triangles and repeats a 32 × 16 wood image over nearly sixteen full-image areas. The water uses two triangles and a separate 32 × 32 image. Two scrolling layers move in opposing horizontal directions over the same little surface. A player looking down into Bellmarket's well sees the coping, inner wall, stone joints and a dark bottom. Give the coping a closed profile, carry its stone around the inside and test a hand on the lip from every side. [Original water movement](https://github.com/zeldaret/oot/blob/14759abf9abb4295de142cdc1452998630ea70fb/src/overlays/actors/ovl_Bg_Spot01_Idomizu/z_bg_spot01_idomizu.c) · [Bellmarket well profile](../../Tools/bellwake/market_art.py)

**Make small things readable in the hand.** The N64 Ocarina of Time item has a separate ten-triangle finger-hole fragment using a 16 × 16 intensity image. Part of the dungeon map covers one 32 × 32 intensity picture across sixteen triangles. When an item fits inside one hand, give the silhouette to thick parts such as the rim and handle. Use a small picture for the thin marks such as a punched hole, rolled-map border or stamped maker's mark. Check the mark at its normal held size, then turn the item toward the light and look at the grip from below. [Ocarina item definitions](https://github.com/zeldaret/oot/blob/14759abf9abb4295de142cdc1452998630ea70fb/assets/xml/objects/object_gi_ocarina.xml) · [Map definitions](https://github.com/zeldaret/oot/blob/14759abf9abb4295de142cdc1452998630ea70fb/assets/xml/objects/object_gi_map.xml)

## People and animals

**Shape a face before painting it.** Look at the brow line, projecting nose and chin at 01:03:03, then the Goron's head and shoulder outline at 02:17:02. Start a resident with rings at the jaw, cheekbone and brow. Use the profile view to set the nose and the depth of the back of the head. Finish the silhouette with a few solid hair locks or a hat brim. Paint the eyes on the front, check them at conversation distance, then look at the same resident from the side. Give each occupation one gesture a player can read from farther away. Ara carries a rule at the mould loft. Nell's boat hook extends beyond the shoulder. [Bellwake resident shapes](../../Tools/bellwake/residents.py) · [Individual designs](../../Tools/bellwake/resident_designs.py)

**Blink with the eye image.** The bazaar shopkeeper has open, half-open and closed 32 × 32 CI8 images. The draw routine selects one with the actor's eye index. The update routine returns to waiting after the blink and varies the interval. In Bellwake's atlas, each face gets two small moving lid pictures with a one-to-one texel mapping at close range. The open eye lives on the portrait. The iris remains visible under the half lid and the eyelash line survives closure. Give residents different phases so a pair of people standing together can blink independently. [Shopkeeper textures](https://github.com/zeldaret/oot/blob/14759abf9abb4295de142cdc1452998630ea70fb/assets/xml/objects/object_ossan.xml) · [Shopkeeper draw routine](https://github.com/zeldaret/oot/blob/14759abf9abb4295de142cdc1452998630ea70fb/src/overlays/actors/ovl_En_Ossan/z_en_ossan.c#L2365) · [Bellwake eye sampling](../../Assets/Shaders/BellwakeEyes.cginc)

**Build the working clothes around the pose.** Set shoulders, elbow and gripping hand before fitting the apron front. Leave enough clearance at the collar, belt and apron that a contrasting shirt cannot poke through on a head turn. Put hems and closures on a full-height painting and map the cuff, leather and hair from smaller swatches. Compare an outfit from the back as well as the dialogue view. A returning character can keep the same collar and coat through a change of location. [Tailoring and portrait painting](../../Tools/bellwake/resident_paint.py)

**Birds.** At 01:38:03 a white Cucco stands out over Link's head against the green village. The source model assigns a 16 × 16 eye, 8 × 16 head feathers, 8 × 8 base feathers and beak, 16 × 16 comb and 32 × 32 tail feathers. Shape the characteristic comb, beak and tail in profile before allocating the fine feather strokes. For Bellmarket's birds, check both the walking body and the distant image against the same ground position while walking towards and away from them. [Cucco paintings](https://github.com/zeldaret/oot/blob/14759abf9abb4295de142cdc1452998630ea70fb/assets/xml/objects/object_niw.xml#L63)

## Ground, foliage, sky and light

**Keep the path continuous.** At 04:23:00 and 08:17:05, darker grass against the wall makes the forest-stage clearing read even beneath its low roof. At 08:32:05 and 08:52:05, fields hold large shapes in colour over a much greater viewing distance. Paint the route and broad shadows onto continuous ground, then put small tufts or embedded grit on it at a consistent metre scale. In Bellwake, four independently painted images for meadow, trail, slate and snow share one RGBA texture. Sampling it once adds the appropriate marks over the large district painting. Far mip levels average back towards the original colour. In Blender, inspect a fork from both approaches. A shared ground surface lets the player put a hand across the junction. [Painted ground brush](../../Tools/bellwake/ground_brush.py) · [Ground shader](../../Assets/Shaders/BellwakeGroundPrint.cginc)

**Separate a crown from the trunk.** Film a tree first against the sky and then against dark rock. The spaces between its crowns should give it an identifiable silhouette. Change the fork height, lean, crown width and location of the darkest cavity between models. The original tree actor selects different display lists and tint values by type. Some tree types have separately submitted opaque and translucent parts. Model an inspectable Bellwake trunk and close the low branches. On grass and cattails, place alpha-cut planes into real soil and preserve the occupied silhouette in the mips. [Tree draw routine](https://github.com/zeldaret/oot/blob/14759abf9abb4295de142cdc1452998630ea70fb/src/overlays/actors/ovl_En_Wood02/z_en_wood02.c#L450) · [Plant placement](../../Tools/bellwake/bankside_plants.py)

**Join the foreground to its surroundings.** At 04:45:01 the lake shore curves across the frame and a distant structure holds the far side of the water. At 11:52:04 the pale bank and the patterned pond surface meet at a definite edge. Keep a strip of continuous land or seabed between the walkable cliff and the distant hills. Have the coastal water continue from its existing perimeter. On a foggy pass, make near ledges overlap distant ones in the player's view while their meshes meet at a shared seam. Check from the highest usable ledge and again after revisiting from the road. [Landscape construction](../../Tools/bellwake/country.py) · [Landscape join checks](../../Tools/bellwake/check_scenery.py)

**Give fire two speeds.** At 02:03:04 the flames rise from two solid torches flanking the grate. At 09:52:01 the flames, stone brackets and central barred door remain identifiable in the same dark view. The torch actor uses two texture-scroll layers and turns its flame towards the camera. For a VR kiln, anchor the logs and glow beneath a real grate. Animate the tips more than the coals. Bellwake's kiln sheet has sixteen frames and each eye sees the same frame. ## Colour, filtering and the moving camera

**A small palette can describe a large sky.** The N64 sunset bank declares four 128 × 64 CI8 pictures, one 128 × 128 picture and a 16 × 8 RGBA16 colour table. At 03:45:02 in the recording, the orange sky makes the crags readable at the end of a dark mountain path. At 12:03:02, rain silhouettes the fence differently against a dark sky. For a Bellwake panorama, paint the light near the horizon first and decide which ridge will stay visible there. Keep the nearest bank in the walking scene so it moves against the panorama as the player leans. Test the same route at midday, dusk and night. [N64 sky pictures and palettes](https://github.com/zeldaret/oot/blob/14759abf9abb4295de142cdc1452998630ea70fb/assets/xml/textures/skyboxes.xml) · [Bellwake sky paintings](../../Tools/bellwake/skies.py)

**Test a texture at several distances.** Many original display-list presets select the N64's filtered texture mode. A 32 × 64 RGBA16 sail uses 4,096 bytes of texels. A 32 × 64 CI8 picture uses 2,048 bytes of indices plus its palette. Either can fill a substantial part of the view. Use a few large shapes to set the material at a distance, then spend the remaining texels on marks that matter up close. A feather on Bellwake's night watch has a ridge and tip in the model and a complete engraving in its picture. Give the paint a continuous axis from shoulder to tip. A collapsed projection can turn the entire face of a mesh into one streak of colour. Bellwake checks the fitted triangles in the exported keeper and firs for that fault. [Original RDP setup presets](https://github.com/zeldaret/oot/blob/14759abf9abb4295de142cdc1452998630ea70fb/src/code/z_rcp.c) · [Bellwake enamel fitting](../../Tools/bellwake/keeper_paint.py) · [Exported surface checks](../../Tools/bellwake/audit.py)

**Budget the details that move.** The windmill musician's chest and pipe fragment uses the shirt painting on 52 triangles, the music-box painting on twelve and an 8 × 8 horn-and-pipe image on 32. The separate horn also carries a 32 × 32 close-up of the upper horn and crank. Plan a musician or keeper around the parts that must move together. The music box can sit on a broad painted case, while a small handle has an actual shaft, grip and pivot. In the final belfry, check the register from below, look past the feather spines and turn around the brass details. [Windmill musician materials and limbs](https://github.com/zeldaret/oot/blob/14759abf9abb4295de142cdc1452998630ea70fb/assets/xml/objects/object_fu.xml) · [Bellwake final keeper and music box](../../Tools/bellwake/quiet_art.py)

## Sources and scope

The model measurements above come from named display-list fragments resolved from the supplied European revision 1 ROM. One square of normalized UV area means one full copy of an image. Summing the absolute triangle areas measures paint coverage, including repeated texels. The references use the matching PAL data and pin the source revision. Timings refer to the supplied 16-hour, 56-minute recording. This pass sampled nine fixed offsets per complete hour, inspected scenes at player height and cross-checked specific visual details against the object declarations and draw code. Bellwake uses its own paintings, Blender models, names and story. [Earlier ROM measurements](../oot-rom-study.md) · [Recording details](ocarina-walkthrough-study.md)
